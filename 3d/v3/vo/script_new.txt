Have you ever wondered where your WhatsApp message goes, the second you hit send?
Let's follow one.
First, your phone scrambles it into code which only you and your friend can read. Not even WhatsApp.
It goes into the nearest tower as radio waves.
The tower turns it into light, and fires it through glass cables under the road.
At the coast, it dives into the Atlantic.
How far do you think it travels down there?
Over six thousand kilometers. In a cable about as thick as a garden hose.
Every seventy kilometers or so, a repeater boosts the light.
Out of the sea, it hits a WhatsApp server. One grey tick. The server has it, but it can't read a word of it.
Then it jumps to your friend's phone, and unlocks. Two grey ticks.
Lagos to London. Under a second.
Blue ticks? That's up to your friend.

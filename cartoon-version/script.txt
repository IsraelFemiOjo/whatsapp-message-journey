What actually happens in the second after you hit send on WhatsApp?
Let's follow one message, from Lagos to London!
First, your phone locks it. Only you and your friend can read it. Not even WhatsApp!
It shoots out as radio waves to the nearest cell tower.
Then it turns into light, and races through glass cables under the road.
At the coast, it dives into the ocean!
Quick guess. How far does it travel down there?
Over six thousand kilometers, in a cable about as thick as a garden hose!
Every seventy kilometers or so, a repeater boosts the light.
Out of the sea, it reaches a WhatsApp server. One grey tick. The server has it, but it can't read it.
Then it jumps to your friend's phone, and unlocks. Two grey ticks!
All of that, in under a second.
